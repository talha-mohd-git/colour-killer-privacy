Colour Killer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Colour Killer, a mobile puzzle game published by the developer of the app, handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Colour Killer does not require an account and does not directly collect, request, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or financial information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The game may store game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, or high scores. This information is used only to provide the game experience and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer by the current version of the app.

3. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Colour Killer does not contain advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, or another third-party service, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is distributed.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally send personal information to third-party service providers. Android and Google Play may process technical information as part of operating the device, distributing the app, security checks, or platform services; those activities are governed by the relevant platform policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Colour Killer does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer through a future version or support channel, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing so the matter can be reviewed.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app's locally stored game data limited to its intended purpose. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact email shown on the Colour Killer Google Play Store listing.
